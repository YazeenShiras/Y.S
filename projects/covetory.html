Overview

Covetory is a fashion brand concept built around the idea “Modern essentials. Quiet confidence.” The visual direction uses a calm palette, editorial typography and product-first layouts rather than a generic storefront template.

The challenge

Create an ecommerce experience that feels premium and distinctive without making the shopping journey complicated. The interface needs to give product photography, sizing and product information room to breathe.

Approach

Build a responsive storefront with reusable product and collection patterns. The broader product roadmap includes guest and signed-in cart behavior, OTP authentication, payment choices and an admin experience. Keep each capability labelled accurately as it moves from planning into implementation.

Selected features

  • Editorial storefront direction
  • Product and collection browsing
  • Guest and signed-in cart flow
  • OTP authentication integration
  • Razorpay and cash-on-delivery checkout planning
  • Admin and inventory workflow planning
