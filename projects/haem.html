Overview

HÆM is a premium bag brand concept with a quiet visual language built around white, sage, beige and earthy neutrals. The product direction includes shoulder, mini and tote silhouettes.

The challenge

Create a distinct identity that feels premium without relying on loud visual elements, and build a product presentation system that can scale across a small collection.

Approach

Use a tactile palette, restrained typography and consistent naming. Design product pages around clear imagery, material details and practical product information.

  • HÆM MINI evening bag concept
  • HÆM TOTE concept
